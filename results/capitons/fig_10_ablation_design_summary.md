# fig_10_ablation_design_summary

Evidence summary for next-model planning. Panel a shows Primary mean MAE for 12 common-algorithm baselines. Panel b shows mean paired split MAE differences for RDKit2D minus PhysChem-10 (negative favors RDKit2D). Panel c shows the validation-selected reference model's fixed-test MAE by train/validation-defined target quartile. These observations motivate hypotheses for future experiments; they do not establish causal mechanisms.
